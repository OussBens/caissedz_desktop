★The rules in numbers

All server-side numbers live in catalog-api; the two thresholds can change in its .env without updating any PC.

3 shops + 20 ticketsover 90 days ⇒ the product is trending
TENDANCE_POSTES_MIN, TENDANCE_TICKETS_MIN
1 000 + 10ranking points per shop that sold it + per ticket (1 000 tickets counted at most)
50 / daytickets counted at most per shop, product and day: one shop can't make a trend alone
5 000best-ranked products offered per section (rayon), pork & alcohol excluded
30 daysof sales a PC sends after a long time offline (the server keeps a 90-day window)
1 shopis enough to add an unknown barcode to the catalogue; known products only get their empty fields filled
2 → 5 · 0a shop-added product starts at 2; +1 / −1 per shop that scans it; confirmed at 5, deleted at 0
CONFIRMATION_*

Rank in a section = barcode lookups × 100 + 50 if Algerian (613…) + 10 with a photo + trending points. Never sent by a PC: prices, quantities, stock, customers.

AFirst sign-in — choosing and downloading sections

On a new PC, after the login and before the setup wizard, for a user who can open Produits.

sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  actor U as Shop owner
  participant D as Desktop
  participant C as catalog-api
  participant B as bens_ds
  U->>D: logs in on a new PC
  D->>C: GET /rayons
  C-->>D: 16 sections: names FR/AR/EN, products offered (≤ 5 000), trending count
  U->>D: picks sections, Apply
  loop each section, page by page (500)
    D->>C: GET /rayons/code/products?page=n
    C-->>D: products: barcodes, name, Arabic name, brand, photo, trending
  end
  Note over D: ONE transaction: a local category per section,
products with no price and no stock (source Catalogue),
a barcode already known is never added twice
  D-->>U: summary: added, % of offered, already there
  D->>B: POST /api/app/caissedz-desktop/catalog-choice
  alt offline
    D->>D: kept, sent at the next check-in
  end
  • Later: nothing downloaded; the screen reopens from Produits › Catalogue BENS.
  • Offline while downloading: message and Retry; nothing is written until everything arrived.
  • A downloaded product never stocked is not an out-of-stock alert.

BEvery launch, online — report, then pull what's new

In the background, without slowing the opening of the app. Only with an active licence.

sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  participant D as Desktop
  participant B as bens_ds
  participant C as catalog-api
  D->>B: check-in (licence, plan, Smart Scans)
  B-->>D: licence up to date
  Note over D: Sheets edited since the last one sent,
tickets per day per EAN barcode since the last day sent (included)
  loop chunks of 4 000 lines, sheets first
    D->>C: POST /tendance {produits, ventes}
X-Device-Id + X-License-Key
    Note over C: licence checked → PC code CD-XXXX-XXXX
    C->>C: sheets: unknown barcode → new product,
known → empty fields filled, logged per PC
    C->>C: sales: per product and day, max 50 a day,
re-sent day REPLACES the old one
    C->>C: recompute 90-day shops, tickets, points, trending flag
+ 300 stale products, purge > 120 days
    C-->>D: {ventes, inconnus, crees, completes}
  end
  Note over D: success → remembers today's date and the last sheet sent
  loop each section downloaded
    D->>C: GET /rayons/code/products?depuis=last check
    C-->>D: products that became trending since then
  end
  Note over D: ONE transaction: adds only those marked trending,
fills missing Arabic names, refreshes the lists
Idempotent: if a report fails midway, everything is sent again next time. Sales of a day replace the earlier count; sheets can only fill empty fields, so nothing is counted twice or overwritten.

COffline, refused, no licence, long absence

The PC never loses data: nothing is marked sent until the server says OK.

  N -- no --> X2[Nothing marked sent

today + last sheet]

  A -- "timeout / 500" --> X3
  M --> P[Pull new trending per section]
  P --> Q{Answer}
  Q -- OK --> W[Add trending only

  Q -- offline --> X4[Next launch, same 'since' day]

  • While offline the PC misses nothing about trending: the next pull asks “since my last successful check”.
  • A product that trended and stopped while the PC was offline is skipped (it's no longer trending).

IConfirming a product a shop added

Only products created because the catalogue didn't know their barcode. Imported products and filled-in sheets don't need it.

flowchart TD
  S([Shop A creates a product
the catalogue didn't know]) --> P[Catalogue product, TO CONFIRM
score 2, not offered in sections]
  P --> SC{Another shop scans
this barcode at the till}
  SC -- "'Oui, c'est ce produit'" --> UP[+1]
  SC -- "'Ce n'est pas ce produit'" --> DN[−1]
  SC -- "closes the dialog" --> P
  UP --> CALC[score = 2 + all votes
one vote per shop, the last one counts
shop A's own vote ignored]
  DN --> CALC
  CALC --> T{Score}
  T -- "5 or more" --> OK[CONFIRMED for good
offered in its section, can trend
no more votes]
  T -- "0 or less" --> DEL[DELETED from the catalogue
barcodes, sales and votes too]
  T -- "1 to 4" --> P
  OK --> LOG[(catalog_contribution:
confirme / supprime)]
  DEL --> LOG
  • The local product of shop A (and of every shop that created it) is never touched: only the shared catalogue changes.
  • While waiting, the product still answers barcode lookups: that's where the other shops vote.
  • Thresholds in catalog-api's .env: CONFIRMATION_DEPART (2), CONFIRMATION_SEUIL (5), CONFIRMATION_SUPPRESSION (0).

FLife of a trending product

The flag is recomputed when a report touches the product, and day by day for the others (the window slides).

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> InCatalogue: imported or added by a shop
  InCatalogue --> Selling: first ticket reported (points added)
  Selling --> Trending: 3+ shops AND 20+ tickets in 90 days
  Trending --> Trending: more sales (same 'since' date)
  Trending --> Selling: window slides below a threshold
  Selling --> InCatalogue: no sale left in 90 days (points back to 0)
  Trending --> OnPCs: PCs with its section pull it (since date)
  OnPCs --> OnPCs: kept forever on the PC
  • Leaving the trending list never deletes a product from a PC: sales, stock and invoices point to it.
  • “Trending since” is the day it became trending; it resets only if it drops and trends again.
