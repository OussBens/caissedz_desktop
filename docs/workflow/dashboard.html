  • Overview: products, sections, offered to PCs, trending, to confirm, Arabic names and photos (%), charts per day over 90 days (tickets, shops, products added, sheets completed), each section’s size and the catalogue’s quality.
  • Sections: the list (order, type, products, offered, trending, to confirm); a section’s names FR/AR/EN, icon, order, on, priority, fallback, keywords (one per line) and its 50 best-ranked products; add a section.
  • Trending: the rule, best sellers, each shop with its client (click: the client’s page), what shops added.
  • To confirm: products shops added, their score, votes and who added them; confirm or delete by hand.
  • Maintenance: re-sort (batches until done, with progress and Stop), recompute trends now (after changing the rule), update the tables (after a deploy).

BRe-sorting from the dashboard

A request can’t last minutes on shared hosting: the dashboard runs short batches one after the other.

sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  actor A as Admin
  participant W as Dashboard
  participant B as bens_ds
  participant C as catalog-api
  A->>W: Maintenance → Re-sort the catalogue
  loop until done (or Stop)
    W->>B: POST /api/admin/catalog/reclassify {from}
    B->>C: POST /admin/reclasser {depuis}
    C->>C: about 15 s of batches of 5 000 products
    C-->>B: {depuis, reste, total, fini}
    B-->>W: {from, left, total, done}
    W-->>A: progress bar, products left
  end
  Note over B: logged: Catalogue re-sort started

CWhen something fails

What the Catalogue page shows.

AnswerMeans
503 not_configuredREV_SERVER_TOKEN or CATALOG_API_URL missing in bens_ds
502 catalog_unreachablecatalog-api down, or its token differs (check both .env)
400 / 404 / 409 catalog_refusedcatalog-api’s own refusal (bad keyword list, unknown section, code already used): its message is shown

  • Overview : produits, rayons, proposés aux postes, tendances, à confirmer, noms arabes et photos (%), graphiques par jour sur 90 jours (tickets, boutiques, produits ajoutés, fiches complétées), la taille de chaque rayon et la qualité du catalogue.
